Good to Know

Questions

Where do we meet?

We'll send the exact dock location and parking details when your trip is confirmed. We run out of the St. Pete and Clearwater area.

What if the weather turns bad?

Safety comes first. If Captain Ron calls the trip for weather, you can reschedule for a later date or cancel free.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancellations are free. Plans change, and we get it. Just call or text as early as you can so we can open the date up for someone else.

How many people can come?

Each trip lists its max group size, and kids count toward the total. Ask if you have a bigger crew and we'll figure it out.

Do you have life jackets for kids?

Yes, in every size from infant to adult. Tell us how many kids are coming and their ages when you book, and the right sizes will be on board.

Can we bring food and drinks?

Absolutely. Bring whatever you like. Please skip glass bottles.

Do we pay up front?

No. Nothing is charged until Captain Ron confirms your trip. Tips for the captain are always appreciated.
