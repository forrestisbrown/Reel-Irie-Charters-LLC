Island Time

Where We Go

Some of the best water in Florida is right out our back door.

Shell Key

Undeveloped barrier island with clear shallows, shelling and dolphins cruising the flats.

Egmont Key

Old fort ruins, a working lighthouse and gopher tortoises at the mouth of Tampa Bay.

Caladesi Island

Regularly ranked among the top beaches in America. Soft sand, no crowds.

Pass-a-Grille

Grass flats and passes loaded with snook, reds and trout. A local favorite.

Fort De Soto

Five interconnected keys with sandbars made for anchoring up and swimming.

The Gulf Reefs

Nearshore wrecks and ledges for grouper, snapper, kingfish and Spanish mackerel.

Good to Know

Questions

Where do we meet?

We'll send the exact dock location and parking details when your trip is confirmed. We run out of the St. Pete and Clearwater area.

What if the weather turns bad?

Safety comes first. If the captain calls the trip for weather, you can reschedule or get a full refund of your deposit.

Do we need fishing licenses?

No. Everyone fishing on a licensed charter is covered.

How many people can come?

Each trip lists its max group size. Kids count toward the total. Ask if you have a bigger crew and we'll figure it out.

Can we keep our fish?

Yes, as long as they're in season and within Florida size and bag limits. We'll clean them at the dock for you.

Can we bring food and drinks?

Absolutely. Bring whatever you like. Please skip glass bottles.

Is there a deposit?

A deposit holds your date once the captain confirms. The balance is due on the day of your trip. Tips for the crew are always appreciated.
